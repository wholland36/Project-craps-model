# Incremental injury research candidate

This candidate is separate from the official NCAAF model. It was requested after three quantitative reviews of broader injury variations. It does not approve betting, change canonical forecasts or edit wagers.

A new absence requires explicit Available in the immediately previous game and Out in the current game. A return requires explicit Out then Available. An omitted name, Exempt, Questionable or Game Time Decision is unresolved. Continuing Out is zero **incremental** shock, not zero player value or proof that the official model fully absorbed that injury. The official model uses EWMA attack/defense updates at .15; this adjacent-status convention is a deliberately simpler proxy, not an exact EWMA availability correction.

Use up to three preceding explicitly Available games within42days and at least48hours before the current cutoff. Estimate passing-attempt share for QB, carry share for HB/FB and reception share for WR/TE against the corresponding whole-team denominator. These are opportunity proxies, not snaps. A return uses documented healthy usage before the absence, rather than zero usage during it. No workload is invented for OL, defenders or specialists; unresolved contributions are omitted while all game rows stay present.

For non-QBs, use a verified replacement if available, otherwise the user-authorized mean of up to three lower-rated same-team/position players; one or two accepted. Exclude all concurrent Out name candidates, including ambiguous rated matches. QB backups must have positive prior passing usage and compatible ratings, and remain projected rather than verified. Shared replacement pools are recorded. Sum all resolved shocks in QB, RUSH and RECEIVE groups, pro-rata capping summed workload at1 within a group. No arbitrary extra depth penalty is introduced.

Fit nonnegative coefficients, with a free intercept and training-only standardization. Select penalties10/100/1000 using chronological inner folds. Include frozen, mean-bias and median-bias baselines; the selector may choose zero injury. The previously examined later117games remain diagnostics, not a new holdout.

The development data have only five training and four later supported games with nonzero shocks. QB features are constant zero and **unidentified**. The constrained RUSH/RECEIVE optimum is the zero boundary. The apparent improvement over the frozen baseline comes from bias correction; the candidate establishes no injury-specific gain.

The separate prospective adapter saves local source bytes and observation receipts, checks code/protocol hashes, reconstructs submitted status/workload/aggregate features, and refuses post-kickoff registration. It preserves separate baseline, selected policy, shadow and mean/median comparators. A receipt journal detects missing, duplicated or modified records; exact final identity and chronology are required. It never writes the official registry. Local capture receipts do not authenticate source authorship or prove that manual source-to-player attribution is correct. Those fields remain explicitly unauthenticated. A caller must review extraction and source identities.

The frozen research horizon covers the rest of season2026 through January28,2027. Preregistered sample-readiness thresholds are100supported games,20nonzero-shock games and8season/type/week blocks. Reaching those counts is not production approval. With current injury coefficients zero, future records principally collect evidence and compare bias baselines; a future refit must be separately frozen and evaluated on still newer games. Never silently train on a prospective evaluation cohort and relabel it untouched.

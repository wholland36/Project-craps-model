# Probability v2 research tools

These functions are isolated research candidates. They neither import nor replace the official model, and they do not authenticate caller-supplied timestamps.

`center_audit(point, residual_pairs, discrete_scores=None)` distinguishes a regression point prediction from the paired-residual continuous distribution mean and, if supplied, the integer score distribution mean. `jointly_center` transfers the joint residual mean into the point exactly once. It preserves every continuous paired draw and therefore is a bookkeeping correction, not a demonstrated forecast improvement. The caller must supply an earlier fit vintage and cannot claim a previously applied bias correction. Whether the declared vintage or bias state is truthful needs an external manifest audit.

`calibrate_joint_support` is an explicit integer joint-score reweighting candidate. Supply existing nonnegative integer score atoms, separate earlier calibration results with observed-at times, signed key margins, and a positive smoothing pseudocount chosen in advance. It shrinks empirical key-category probabilities toward the support's category probabilities; it cannot manufacture absent score atoms and rejects any observed unsupported category. All markets must use the same returned candidate through `weighted_outcome_masses`. This preserves a single coherent joint distribution across moneylines, spreads and totals, but is not evidence that global margin-category weighting is conditionally calibrated for a particular matchup. Do not apply this calibration separately to markets or fit it to test outcomes. No numerical key weights were fit or activated in this implementation.

`proper_scores` computes three-way W/P/L Brier and log loss plus conditional nonpush scores. Zero probability of an observed outcome yields infinite log loss, never hidden clipping. No-vig paired prices identify a conditional benchmark only, not sportsbook push mass. `same_book_novig` checks explicit same-book/event/time/scope/complementary sides and lines and pregame quote clocks; this is structural validation, not independent source authentication. Monetary EV is win mass times profit odds minus loss mass.

`offered_line_push_diagnostic` uses actual supplied lines and canonicalizes spread sides to the home handicap; it does not merge home -3 with away -3. It averages quotes within each event/line before equal event weighting. `event_balanced` prevents book-rich games dominating. `week_block_ci` resamples whole season/calendar-week blocks and refuses an interval with fewer than eight usable blocks; the eight-block threshold is an explicit guard, not a guarantee of precision. Repeated teams and only four seasons remain limitations. Null metrics and infinite log losses need explicit reporting.

`fixed_policy_roi` audits externally frozen tickets, not retrospective price selection. It allows one ticket per event/market, refunds pushes, requires pregame decisions and externally attested source authentication, and returns no invented confidence interval. A later integration must preregister the ticket policy, stake rules, dependence design and independent source evidence.

The November 5 diagnostic only reads already completed private historical predictions, verifies their original hash, and produces aggregate reports. It performs no model refit, parameter search, paid calls, live execution or official forecast changes. Provider timestamps remain unauthenticated and fixed-policy executable ROI is NOT_COMPUTED.

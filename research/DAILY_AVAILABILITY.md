# Daily availability challenger adapter

Research only. `collect(sport,event,cutoff,private_dir)` supports NCAAF, NFL and men's NCAAB. `event` requires `event_id`, `kickoff`, `home_id`, `away_id`; NCAAF may supply `conferences` from SEC/ACC/B12/B10. The cutoff must still be in the future and before kickoff. Capture runs once per URL per UTC date, including cached failures; no paid endpoints or retries. Raw summaries can contain odds and remain private. Receipts bind exact raw SHA and real observation clocks. Public ESPN summary, team roster and injury endpoints are candidates, not proof of injury-report completeness; endpoints can fail or contain no current injury data. Scoped NCAAF conference archive captures use the existing public HD Intelligence endpoint. NFL game-status details require typed verified player report records, not inference from roster absence.

The optional private inbox is `private_dir/inbox/SPORT_EVENT_YYYY-MM-DD.json` with `players` list. Each row requires sport/event/team/player identities, side, explicit Available/Out, source SHA matching a captured source, source publication before its observation before cutoff with at most24hours age, verified role/workload/replacement, finite signed_loss, group and workload. These verification fields are caller attestations requiring actual evidence, not independent authentication. NFL requires NFL_INDEPENDENT_POINTS_PER_FULL_ROLE; NCAAB requires NCAAB_INDEPENDENT_POINTS_PER_FULL_ROLE and minutes_share or usage_share. No EA football scale is assigned to basketball. Football QB rows require qb_role_verified. Uncertain statuses remain unscored scenarios. Unknown players remain in `unknown_contributions`; games are retained. Missing typed inbox does not mean healthy.

`adjust(base_point,context,model)` excludes bias intercepts. The NCAAF frozen candidate must match its known SHA; its three zero injury coefficients are reported with identification limits and no improved point forecast. Generic NFL/NCAAB values require independently_trained, sport, trained_before, coefficients, identified_groups and a canonical JSON SHA256 over the complete model excluding model_sha256 (sort_keys=True,separators=(',',':')). This verifies binding, not the scientific claim of independent training; review that evidence separately. Missing coefficients return NOT_COMPUTED with no copied baseline passed off as a challenger. Known contributions only are scored, with unresolved counts preserved. This adapter neither fits coefficients nor changes official models, Elo, forecasts, Sheets or wagers.

Canonical NFL event/team keys may differ from ESPN keys. Supply espn_event_id, home_espn_id, away_espn_id for source URLs; canonical event/team IDs remain in the typed inbox and returned context. Fixtures more than24hours away are not fetched. The root engine must verify final observed clocks do not exceed actual forecast issuance. No-model NCAAF requests use the known frozen candidate only when its expected persistent path exists and SHA matches; otherwise return NOT_COMPUTED.

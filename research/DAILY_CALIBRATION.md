# Frozen daily calibration challenger

This is an actual fitted, separate research challenger for NCAAF, NFL and NCAAB. It does not replace official artifacts. Fitting minimizes the sum of conditional categorical negative log likelihood plus a fixed ridge penalty of 10/2 times squared offsets. Each game's own forecast category probabilities are the likelihood offsets. This is not a ratio of unconditional observed and predicted averages. No hyperparameter grid or later-result selection is performed.

Football categories are signed exact margins -7,-3,+3,+7 and other; NFL adds an exact tie category. Basketball uses negative and positive margins of 1–3, 4–10 and 11+ points. Basketball has no football key-number assumption. The fitted offsets exponentially reweight existing integer joint home/away score atoms. Every market must settle from that same weighted support. No new scores are invented. Tied atoms are retained for NFL and rejected for NCAA full-game support; the caller must remove unsupported NCAA tied draws before applying. The candidate cannot repair a category with zero original support.

Interface: `fit(rows, sport, fit_cutoff)` and `apply(scores, artifact)`. Fit rows require unique event IDs, aware kickoff/result-observed timestamps, actual integer margins and normalized per-game forecast category probabilities. At least 30 eligible games are required; unavailable future outcomes, duplicates and observed categories outside support fail. Apply returns a dict with `scores` and `weights`, compatible with probability_v2 weighted outcome scoring. The daily caller must verify artifact/code/base-model hashes and ensure its forecast time is after the fitted cutoff; the helper does not independently authenticate source evidence.

Actual frozen fits use archived 2025 NFL/NCAAF original-architecture out-of-sample fold predictions (272/808 games), and 2026-labelled NCAAB season predictions (6,300 games). For basketball, the test fold trains through2024 and calibrates residuals on2025; category probabilities are reconstructed using that fold's residual pairs and each saved predicted margin/total, with integer rounding and tied draws removed. The official basketball artifact trained later is not applied backward. All three use historical reconstructed data, not original pregame issuance. Original source publication clocks remain unauthenticated.
